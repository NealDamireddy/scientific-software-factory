# Comparison protocol

One fresh agent answers all four historical requests without seeing the complete source threads. The control and applied-skill arms have three reviewers each, with matched case order in pairs (A/B/C/D, C/D/A/B, D/B/A/C). Reviewers receive the same decision-time packets, the same 145-word limit per case and the same instruction to give the investigator a decision and its basis. No web, other agents, adjudication, or later files are authorized. Arms differ only in access and direction to read Data intake and provenance, Claim-to-evidence review and Evidence summary. The assisted reviewers are asked to confirm the three file reads separately from their answers.

Two additional fresh sessions initially had optional access to the skill library. Both reported that they consulted no skills. Their answers are retained as a separate discovery check, excluded from the primary effect comparison. The explicit-reading arm was introduced after those reports of non-use and before scoring. The packets, answer deadlines, historical key and per-case rubric remained fixed. Assignment to conditions was by dispatch, not randomized.

The scorer receives anonymous answers, packets and frozen rubric without arm mapping. The primary measure is decision correctness at cutoff (one point per case). Two further evidence checks per case measure support and handling of uncertainty. Hindsight is not part of the reward. Separately compare the recorded outcome to the as-of decision, distinguishing conditional advice from forecast of specific later findings. Report condition and case-level totals and individual checks. No human scientist adjudicated these cases for this experiment.
