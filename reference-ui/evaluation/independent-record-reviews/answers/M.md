### Formaldehyde.csv — hold the unknown result

The six standards (lines 2–7) support the fit OD = 0.005086 + 0.876286 × carbohydrate volume in mL, with R² = 0.99905. The proposed OD of 0.800 exceeds the observed range of 0.086–0.782. Inverting the fit would extrapolate to 0.907 mL, beyond the highest standard of 0.900 mL. R² describes the fit to those standards. Quantifying the unknown requires a validated range covering its response or a documented dilution and an in-range remeasurement. Reporting mg/L also requires a calibration in mass concentration or a documented conversion from volume using stock concentration and preparation volumes.
