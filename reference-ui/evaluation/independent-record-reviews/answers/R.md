### Formaldehyde

**Withhold the unknown result.** The six standards give the fitted equation optical density = 0.005086 + 0.876286 × carbohydrate volume (mL), with R² = 0.99905. An optical density of 0.800 exceeds the highest standard, 0.782 at 0.900 mL. Inverting the fit gives 0.907 mL by extrapolation. The fit alone cannot establish accuracy there. Measure a dilution within the calibration range or validate an extended range. Reporting mg/L also requires a mass-based calibration or the stock concentration and relevant sample volume and dilution information.

### ChickWeight

**Revise the cohort and gain.** Of 20 Diet 1 chicks weighed at birth, 16 had a day-21 weight. Chicks 8, 15, 16 and 18 lack that endpoint. The draft subtracts the birth mean for all 20 chicks (41.40 g) from the day-21 mean for the 16 measured chicks (177.75 g). For those same 16 chicks, the birth mean was 41.5625 g and the mean individual gain was 136.1875 g, or **136.19 g**. The four missing endpoints leave the mean gain for all 20 undetermined.

### attenu

**Revise the event count, mean and station claim.** The 182 acceleration readings come from 23 distinct earthquake IDs. Event 19 alone contributes 38 readings. Averaging all rows gives 0.1542 g and weights each event in proportion to its reading count. Averaging the readings within each earthquake, then giving each of the 23 event means equal weight, gives **0.1329 g**. Sixteen readings have a blank station ID, leaving 166 with an identified station. The records give no basis for assuming statistical independence.
