### Formaldehyde — revise before release

Across the six standards, optical density = 0.005086 + 0.876286 × carbohydrate volume (mL), with R² = 0.99905. The standards span 0.100–0.900 mL and optical densities of 0.086–0.782. Inverting the fit at 0.800 gives 0.9071 mL by extrapolation. The high R² describes the fit within the standards and does not validate quantification at 0.800. Recalibrate with standards bracketing the unknown response. Reporting mg/L also requires a known relationship between carbohydrate volume and mass concentration, which these records do not supply.

### ChickWeight — revise before release

Diet 1 had 20 chicks at birth and 16 with day-21 weights. Chicks 8, 15, 16, and 18 had their last recorded weights on days 20, 14, 12, and 2, respectively. The reported 136.35 g comes from subtracting the birth mean for all 20 chicks (41.40 g) from the day-21 mean for 16 (177.75 g). Those same 16 chicks averaged 41.5625 g at birth, giving a mean paired gain of 136.1875 g (136.19 g). The four missing day-21 weights prevent calculating an observed mean individual gain for the full cohort.

### attenu — revise before release

The 182 peak acceleration readings come from 23 distinct earthquakes, with 1–38 readings per event. Event 19 alone contributed 38 readings. Averaging all 182 readings gives 0.1542 g and gives events with more readings greater weight. Averaging within each event and then averaging the 23 event means gives 0.1329 g with equal earthquake weights. Readings from the same earthquake share an event and cannot be counted as independent earthquakes. Sixteen readings lack a station ID, including the 0.280 g reading for event 16 on CSV line 80. Those readings still have event IDs and are included in both means.
