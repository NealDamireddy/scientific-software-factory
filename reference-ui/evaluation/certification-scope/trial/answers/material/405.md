The XRF result expresses bulk magnesium on an oxide basis. Periclase is a specific crystalline phase. The four XRF vial results in NIST SP 260-204 Table 3 each count once. Their mean is (4.35 + 4.40 + 4.39 + 4.33) / 4 = 4.3675 mass %, which rounds to 4.37. The reported 0.70 percentage points comes from the periclase standard uncertainty of 0.35 multiplied by the coverage factor of two. The archive supplies no basis for using that uncertainty as a control-chart allowance for either method.

The April report’s abstract describes periclase as a diffraction-only reference value. By the 15 September decision date, the 9 July 2021 certificate listed periclase as certified on the combined basis of powder diffraction and electron microscopy with image analysis. Use the July certificate status for the diffraction entry.
