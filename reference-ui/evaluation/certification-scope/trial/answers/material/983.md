Reject the proposed change. Use two constituent IDs for NIST SRM 2686b Portland Cement Clinker. XRF reports bulk magnesium as MgO, while the diffraction chart tracks the mass fraction of the crystalline phase periclase.

| Chart | Constituent | Assigned value | Status and uncertainty |
|---|---|---:|---|
| XRF | Bulk MgO, reported as oxide | 4.37 mass % | NIST information value. No uncertainty or chart allowance is supplied for this entry. |
| Diffraction | Periclase phase | 3.31 mass % | NIST certified phase abundance. Expanded uncertainty is ±0.70 percentage points (k = 2). |

The XRF mean gives one vote to each of the four specimens in NIST SP 260-204, §4.2.1, Table 3. Their values (4.35, 4.40, 4.39 and 4.33 mass %) average 4.3675 mass %, which rounds to 4.37. Table 3 of the 9 July 2021 certificate lists that value for information only and says information values cannot establish metrological traceability.

The April report’s abstract describes the diffraction-only results as reference values. For the 15 September decision, Table 1 of the 9 July 2021 certificate is the issuing record. It certifies periclase at 3.31 mass % on the combined basis of powder diffraction and electron microscopy/image analysis. The report’s Table 2 gives the same consensus mean and a standard uncertainty of 0.35 percentage points, which doubles to 0.70 at k = 2.

Record ±0.70 as the expanded uncertainty of the certified periclase value. The archive supplies no basis for a shared control limit. Reconsider release once the import preserves the two quantities and the chart owner has a documented basis for any method-specific control limits.
