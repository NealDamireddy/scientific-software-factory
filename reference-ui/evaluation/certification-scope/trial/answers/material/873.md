The XRF result is the equal-weight mean of the four clinker specimens reported in NIST SP 260-204, §4.2.1, Table 3. The values 4.35%, 4.40%, 4.39%, and 4.33% average to 4.3675%, which rounds to 4.37%. The July certificate lists it as bulk chemistry information in Table 3.

The April report’s abstract describes periclase as a diffraction-only reference value. By 15 September 2021, the 9 July Certificate of Analysis listed periclase as certified in Table 1, based on combined powder diffraction and electron microscopy/image analysis. The report’s periclase consensus mean is also 3.31%, and its standard uncertainty of 0.35 percentage points doubles to 0.70 at k = 2. That uncertainty applies to the certified phase abundance. Release the two entries separately and require a documented basis for any operational chart limits.
