## USGS Open-File Report 2001-287

USGS distributed six standard reference samples to laboratories in its interlaboratory testing program in April 2001. The M-158 water sample was used for major constituents. The publication evaluates submitted results and summarizes observations by sample, analyte, and laboratory. Participants are identified by number.

The statistical methods section defines the most probable value (MPV) as the median of reported results. F-pseudosigma is a resistant measure of their dispersion computed from the upper and lower fourths. Where this spread falls below 5% of the MPV, USGS uses 5% of the MPV as the rating criterion. An absolute score from 1.01 through 1.50 earns performance rating 2, satisfactory.
