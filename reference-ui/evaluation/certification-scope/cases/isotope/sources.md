The material and analytical records are real. The register draft, batch request and 0.25 mg proposal are illustrative. The assigned values, uncertainty data, portions and dates are selected transcriptions or condensed summaries. These files cover only the supplied material and experiments. The exercise uses the issuer sheet dated 25 September 2025.

- Records 01 and 02 reproduce selected facts from the International Atomic Energy Agency’s IAEA-600 caffeine reference sheet, revision 0. Pages 1–2 give the two value classifications. Page 3 addresses scale normalization and page 4 gives intended use and recommended minimum portions. The issuer's catalogue independently reproduces the tables and scale labels. - Record 03 selects the caffeine row from Table 1 of the primary study *Experimental assessment of elemental analyzer isotope ratio mass spectrometry normalization methodologies for environmental stable isotopes*. The table distinguishes literature values reported for the materials from the authors’ observed precision. Its interval headings are retained.
