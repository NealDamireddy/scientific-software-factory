# Sources and cutoff

The control-chart migration and proposed data mapping are illustrative. All measurement values, dates, classifications and conflicting descriptions in the records come from the following public NIST publications available by 15 September 2021. The CSVs are transcriptions of selected rows and columns. Empty uncertainty cells denote an unreported value in the cited table.

- NIST, certificate issued 9 July 2021 for SRM 2686b Portland Cement Clinker, pages 1–2, tables 1–3. [Certificate of analysis](https://tsapps.nist.gov/srmext/certificates/2686b.pdf)
- P. Stutzman, L. Mundy and A. Heckert, NIST SP 260-204, April 2021, abstract, §4.1 table 2 and §4.2.1 table 3. [Characterization report](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.SP.260-204)
