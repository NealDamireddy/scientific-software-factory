# Selected document notes

NIST SP 260-204 was published in April 2021. Its abstract groups periclase with three phases described there as reference values based on diffraction alone. Section 4.2.1 describes an outside laboratory measuring four clinker specimens by X-ray fluorescence and reporting their bulk composition as oxides.

Page 1 of the 9 July 2021 certificate describes the combined powder diffraction and electron microscopy/image-analysis basis of the certified phase values. The reported expanded uncertainty uses a coverage factor of two. The certificate says that its information values cannot establish metrological traceability.
