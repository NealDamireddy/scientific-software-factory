These are unfiltered CSV exports in published row order from R datasets 4.5.2. The CSV header occupies line 1. Empty fields represent missing values.

Formaldehyde is a spectrophotometric standard-curve dataset. carb = carbohydrate volume in mL. optden = optical density. A simple linear fit in the draft means ordinary least squares with an intercept, optden as response and carb as predictor.
ChickWeight records body weight in grams (weight), days since birth (Time), unique chick ID (Chick), and assigned diet group (Diet). Birds were scheduled for measurement at birth, every other day through day 20, and on day 21.
attenu records earthquake ID (event), moment magnitude (mag), recording station ID (station), station-to-hypocenter distance in km (dist), and peak acceleration in g (accel).
