# NIST SP 260-212, September 2021

Editorial abstract of selected method notes. The transcribed CSVs contain selected rows.

- Section 3.2 describes the ICP-MS survey of two bilirubin samples. The laboratory measured SRM 1643f (water) alongside the samples. The account attributes problems measuring calcium and potassium to background.
- Tables 6 and 7 use superscript b for elements failing the quality criteria. The authors give intervals for their uncertainties. The discussion compares the control scores with 2.
- Section 3.3 describes WDXRF measurements of a 48.19 mg portion of 916b and a 50.19 mg portion of SRM 1566b (oyster tissue). The table assigns expanded uncertainties of 40% of the measured estimates.
- Following Table 8, the authors identify calcium and magnesium as the elements affected by the ICP-MS quality problem. They describe the WDXRF estimate for these as probably more accurate. They also compare the measured chlorine with chloroform estimated by NMR.
