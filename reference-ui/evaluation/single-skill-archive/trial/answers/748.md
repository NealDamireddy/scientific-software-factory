> SRM 916b has a certified unconjugated bilirubin mass fraction of 94.7%, with an expanded uncertainty of 0.7 percentage points (approximately 95% coverage), determined by quantitative proton NMR. The WDXRF survey estimated calcium at 770 ± 310 mg/kg and magnesium at 60 ± 24 mg/kg (expanded uncertainties). These are noncertified estimates. The certificate reports approximately 0.6% total elemental impurities from the elemental survey. [Certificate, pp. 1–2; SP 260-212, Table 8]

ICP-MS Tables 6–7 flag **calcium and potassium**. The report attributes their measurement problems to background. Their control |z| scores were 8.53 and 4.15, respectively, against a comparison threshold of 2. Magnesium was unflagged (|z| = 0.47), and both methods estimated it at 60 mg/kg. WDXRF was a separate measurement with its own control. Its calcium, magnesium and potassium control scores were 1.17, 0.59 and 0.93. [SP 260-212, §§3.2–3.3, Tables 6–8]

The full WDXRF sum was 5,640 ± 2,260 mg/kg (0.564% ± 0.226 percentage points), consistent with the approximate 0.6% survey figure. The certified value came directly from NMR. The elemental survey cannot independently confirm it by difference because it does not account for all impurities. The certificate separately reports volatile impurities below 0.8%, and the report compares elemental chlorine with chloroform estimated by NMR. [Certificate, pp. 1–2; SP 260-212, §3.3, Table 8 and following discussion]
