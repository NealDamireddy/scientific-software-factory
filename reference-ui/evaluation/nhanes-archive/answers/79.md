**Release and method selection.** Paths are relative to the archive. In each cycle the matching `.htm` codebook identifies the transport file, total, units and frequency. Its “Laboratory Method Files” section points to the corresponding manual.

- **2005–06.** `2005/VID_D.htm` was revised in October 2015. Its manual `2005/methods/VID_D_met_Vitamin_D.pdf` identifies the DiaSorin assay on the cover and the equilibrium radioimmunoassay (RIA) in §1. The release table on printed p. 1 labels the total “Vitamin D LCMSMS”. The October 2015 `notices/AnalyticalNote.htm` resolves the apparent discrepancy. CDC remeasured a subset of banked specimens to fit bridging equations. For this cycle the LC-MS/MS-equivalent prediction is 8.36753 + 0.97012 × original unharmonized RIA, both in nmol/L. CDC recommends the already-standardized release for trends. The November 2010 notice and release (`notices/RIAMethod.htm`, `2005/VIDRIA_D.htm`, `2005/VIDRIA_D.xpt`) instead document a QC-drift-adjusted RIA series. Its `LBDVID` has the same 8,306 observations and a median of 22 ng/mL. Neither converting that median's units nor applying the bridge to those adjusted values reproduces the prescribed procedure.
- **2009–10 and 2013–14.** The methodology and analytic notes in `2009/VID_F.htm` and `2013/VID_H.htm` describe separate measurements of the metabolites and define the total in molar units. Published component rounding can affect a reconstructed sum. Use the released total as supplied. The covers and release tables in `2009/methods/VID_F_met_vitamin_D.pdf` and `2013/methods/VID_H_MET.pdf` identify UHPLC-MS/MS methods **4027.03** and **4027.06**, respectively. Both covers give the same creation date (09/02/2010). The H codebook’s statement of no change in method, equipment or site in that cycle cannot establish identical document revisions over time. The nearby 2011 manual (`2011/methods/VID_G_met_Vitamin_D.pdf`) also carries 4027.03 and belongs to another cycle.

**Individual result.** Filtering `2013/VID_H.xpt` by respondent identifier `SEQN == 73557` yields exactly one record (row 1). Its published `LBXVIDMS` is **28.9 nmol/L**, with total detection comment `LBDVIDLC = 0` (at or above the detection limit). D2 is 1.45 (`LBDVD2LC = 1`), D3 is 27.5 (`LBDVD3LC = 0`), and the excluded epimer is 1.16 (`LBDVE3LC = 1`), all in nmol/L. The H codebook’s detection-limits table and comment-code entries explain the D2 and epimer fill values as 2.05/√2 and 1.64/√2, respectively, rounded. The displayed D2 and D3 sum to 28.95. The release retains 28.9. The same respondent also appears in `2013/TST_H.xpt` and `2013/FOLATE_H.xpt`. Their associated manuals identify steroid hormones and folate, respectively. A shared respondent ID across panels cannot identify the vitamin D batch.

**Certification stops at the public row and its cycle documentation.** The codebook links the H manual to the release. Its printed p. 1 maps `VID_H` and `LBXVIDMS` to total vitamin D. The codebook describes reported assays as meeting laboratory QA criteria. The manual documents specimen tracking (§§3, 18), run-level acceptance rules (§10) and aggregate control-pool statistics and charts (§19, pp. 26–28). A detection comment of zero describes the analyte’s detection status. Neither it nor the pooled QC charts identify this specimen’s run. The public transport row contains only the respondent ID, concentrations and detection flags. To certify the exact procedure and passing batch for 73557 would require the laboratory’s respondent-to-specimen/run linkage, the revision in force for that run, and its approved QC record with control results and disposition, including any rerun. The archive supports neither individual certification nor a conclusion that the batch failed.
