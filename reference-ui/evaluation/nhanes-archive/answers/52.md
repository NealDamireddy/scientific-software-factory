**Basis for comparability.** The October 2015 CDC analytical note recommends the predicted LC-MS/MS-equivalent release for 2001–06. CDC remeasured a bridging subset and fitted the 2005–06 equation, equivalent = 8.36753 + 0.97012 × original unharmonized RIA, in nmol/L. The release already contains the prediction. The 2005 manual identifies the serum DiaSorin 25-OH-D assay even though its release table labels the output “Vitamin D LCMSMS”. The November 2010 RIA release, `2005/VIDRIA_D.xpt:LBDVID`, has the same nonmissing count but a median of 22.0 **ng/mL**. It adjusted for assay drift. Unit conversion alone cannot implement the bridging regression, and the drift-adjusted field is the wrong input to that equation. Sources: `notices/AnalyticalNote.htm`, Data Advisory and “CDC 25(OH)D assay, bridging study, and regression equations”; `2005/VID_D.htm`, Methodology, Method Files and codebook; `2005/methods/VID_D_met_Vitamin_D.pdf`, cover and public release table; `2005/VIDRIA_D.htm`, Analytic Notes; `notices/RIAMethod.htm`, RIA-Adjusted Values.

**Method revisions.** The “Laboratory Method Files” links in `2009/VID_F.htm` and `2013/VID_H.htm` identify the cycle-specific manuals. The cover and release table of `2009/methods/VID_F_met_vitamin_D.pdf` identify method **4027.03** for VID_F. Those of `2013/methods/VID_H_MET.pdf` identify **4027.06** for VID_H. Both covers give the same creation date, 09/02/2010. The 2013 codebook's statement of no change to method, equipment or site within that cycle cannot establish identical revisions across cycles. The neighboring `2011/methods/VID_G_met_vitamin_D.pdf` also says 4027.03 and identifies 2011–12 on its release page.

**Record 73557.** In `2013/VID_H.xpt`, the unique row with respondent identifier `SEQN=73557` (first data row) reports `LBXVIDMS=28.9` nmol/L. The codebook defines `LBDVIDLC=0` as at or above the detection limit. D2 was released as 1.45 (`LBXVD2MS`, flag 1), D3 as 27.5 (`LBXVD3MS`, flag 0), and the excluded epimer as 1.16 (`LBXVE3MS`, flag 1), all in nmol/L. The flag-1 values are fills at the respective lower detection limit divided by √2. Use the published total because released components are rounded. The same respondent identifier occurs in `2013/FOLATE_H.xpt` and `2013/TST_H.xpt` for other tests. The vitamin D file and analyte field identify this observation. Sources: `2013/VID_H.htm`, Analytic Notes, Detection Limits and field-specific codebook entries.

**Withhold individual revision and batch-QC certification.** The 2013 codebook documents release-wide QA performance. The linked manual describes the sample ID, raw instrument/batch files and LIMS approval (§3), specifies control acceptance rules (§10), and presents cycle-level QC charts and lot summaries (§19, printed pp. 26–28). The public row provides only SEQN, concentrations and detection-limit flags. Certification requires a crosswalk from this respondent and specimen to the acquisition/run and batch, a contemporaneous record identifying the controlled procedure revision used, and that batch's control readings, acceptance limits and approved disposition. The supplied archive has no such linked record. Batch acceptance remains unverified, with no evidence here of batch failure.
