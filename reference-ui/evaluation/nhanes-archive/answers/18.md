**Release selection.** The 2005–06 laboratory assayed serum with the DiaSorin radioimmunoassay (RIA). CDC reanalyzed a subset of banked sera in a bridging study and published regression-predicted LC–MS/MS equivalents on a scale traceable to NIST reference materials. The cycle equation is 8.36753 + 0.97012 × original, unharmonized RIA, with both quantities in nmol/L. The table uses the already standardized release. The October 2015 analytical note supersedes the November 2010 RIA guidance. The archived `2005/VIDRIA_D.xpt` has the same 8,306 nonmissing observations, but its `LBDVID` median is 22 ng/mL on the QC drift-harmonized RIA scale. Unit conversion would leave that scale difference intact. The 2005 manual’s release table labels `LBDVIDMS` “Vitamin D LCMSMS” while its cover and the codebook document the actual RIA assay. Sources: `2005/VID_D.htm`, Laboratory Methodology and Analytic Notes; `2005/methods/VID_D_met_Vitamin_D.pdf`, cover and Public Release Data Set Information; `notices/AnalyticalNote.htm`, Background and bridging equations; `2005/VIDRIA_D.htm`, Analytic Notes; `notices/RIAMethod.htm`.

**Later methods.** Both later releases define total as 25(OH)D2 + 25(OH)D3 in molar units, excluding the separately measured C3 epimer. Use the released total because rounding can make displayed components sum differently. The cycle-linked manuals identify **4027.03** for 2009–10 and **4027.06** for 2013–14. Both covers carry the creation date 09/02/2010. Their calibration sections document 4% albumin in phosphate-buffered saline in the earlier manual and solvent-based calibration with 70% methanol/water instituted in 2013 in the later manual. The latter reports comparable calibration performance. The 2013–14 codebook’s general “no changes” statement cannot establish an identical revision across those cycles. The neighboring 2007–08 and 2011–12 manuals also show 4027.03, but each pertains to its own release. Sources: `2009/VID_F.htm` and `2013/VID_H.htm`, Laboratory Methodology, Laboratory Method Files and Analytic Notes; `2009/methods/VID_F_met_vitamin_D.pdf` and `2013/methods/VID_H_MET.pdf`, covers, release tables and §7; `2007/methods/VID_E_met_Vitamin_D.pdf` and `2011/methods/VID_G_met_Vitamin_D.pdf`, covers.

**Individual result.** The unique respondent row `2013/VID_H.xpt[SEQN = 73557]` reports **28.9 nmol/L** in `LBXVIDMS` with `LBDVIDLC=0`. D3 is 27.5 (`LBXVD3MS`, flag 0), D2 is 1.45 (`LBXVD2MS`, flag 1), and the excluded epimer is 1.16 (`LBXVE3MS`, flag 1), all in nmol/L. Flag 0 means at or above the detection limit. Flag 1 denotes a published fill value equal to the lower detection limit divided by √2 (D2, 2.05/√2; epimer, 1.64/√2). The displayed D2 and D3 sum to 28.95. Preserve the released 28.9. These flags describe detection, not batch QC. Source: `2013/VID_H.htm`, Analytic Notes, detection-limit table and codebook for the named variables.
