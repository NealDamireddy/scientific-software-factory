## Basis for the two reviews

Report lines are identified in notebook/report-lines.csv. In notebook/journal.jsonl, signed accession and physical transfer entries establish specimen custody. Signed result selections identify receipts. Notebook/export-notes.md gives an explicit signed same-line amendment precedence. Journal times below are converted from UTC−07:00. The acquisitions, controls and dispositions cited below are rows of the runs, controls and dispositions tables in instrument/acquisitions.sqlite. Under instrument/archive-notes.md, controls are selected by instrument serial, batch UID and level, using the latest signed event posted by each cutoff. Dispositions must also be signed. Neither the display label nor a matching numeric result uniquely identifies a measurement.
