# Caffeine in coffee by HPLC · simulated lab protocol

Report caffeine in the original coffee in mg/L. The input is the integrated caffeine peak area for each injection. Peak identification and integration are complete. There is one injection per row in this simulated batch.

Calibrate the detector response against the standards run with this batch. Use unweighted ordinary least squares with peak area as the response and known vial concentration (mg/L) as the predictor. Retain the fitted intercept. Obtain the concentration in each injected vial from its measured area. The dilution column gives the ratio of final volume to original sample volume. Correct each vial concentration to its original-sample basis using that row's dilution. A dilution of one is an undiluted sample.

Use the calibration interval of the batch. Withhold results for readings outside the range of standard signals. Positive dilutions are required. Report in mg/L without rounding intermediate results. Do not infer mass per serving or caffeine per mass of coffee. The reviewer supplies the independent control certificate separately.
