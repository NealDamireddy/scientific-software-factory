# Density of liquids · simulated lab protocol

Report the mass of one millilitre of each simulated liquid at 20 °C in g/mL. For each specimen, the balance gives the net liquid mass in grams after the empty container was tared. The volume column records the actual dispensed volume in millilitres and must be positive. Express the measured mass per unit of the specimen's own volume.

The independently characterized check liquid provides a quality check. This direct measurement uses no fitted calibration or standard rows. Sample volumes may differ, so the mass alone is not a calibration-range criterion. Report the unrounded density. Temperature correction, buoyancy correction and physical-instrument validation are outside this simulation.
